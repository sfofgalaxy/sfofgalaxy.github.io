Our paper [Protected Exercise for Bridgeless Cross-Chain Options Without Upfront Holder Collateral](https://doi.org/10.1109/tdsc.2026.3746660) has been published in **IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC)**.

The work studies a bridgeless cross-chain option protocol in which holders do not need to post collateral when the option is established. Protected exercise is enforced through a writer-side guarantee, so the holder can be compensated if exercise is not completed, while Double-Authentication-Preventing Signatures reduce position-transfer latency.
